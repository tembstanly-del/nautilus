## Course Overview

This comprehensive course provides training in:

- **Pharmaceutical Compounding** - Theory and practice
- **Pharmaceutical Calculations** - Conceptual approach
- **Dosage Forms** - Liquids, semi-solids, solids, sterile
- **Quality Assurance** - Testing and documentation
- **Professional Practice** - Dispensing and patient care

**Goal:** Develop competent, confident compounding practitioners

---

## Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. ✓ **Understand** principles and practices of pharmaceutical compounding
2. ✓ **Perform** pharmaceutical calculations accurately
3. ✓ **Select** appropriate equipment and techniques
4. ✓ **Apply** quality control measures
5. ✓ **Follow** proper documentation and regulatory requirements
6. ✓ **Ensure** patient safety through proper labeling and counseling
7. ✓ **Demonstrate** competency in aseptic technique (when required)

# Course Structure

8 Modules Over 8 Weeks

---

## Module 1: Introduction to Pharmaceutical Compounding

**Week 1**

**Topics:**
- History and importance of compounding
- Legal and regulatory framework
- USP chapters 795 and 797
- Equipment and facilities
- Professional responsibilities

**Activities:**
- Facility tour
- Equipment identification
- Review of SOPs

---

## Module 2: Pharmaceutical Calculations

**Week 2**

**Topics:**
- Fundamental calculation concepts
- Dimensional analysis
- Dosage calculations
- Concentration and dilution
- Percentage strength and ratio strength
- Alligation methods

**Activities:**
- Calculation workshops
- Practice problems
- Error prevention exercises

---

## Module 3: Dosage Forms - Liquids

**Week 3**

**Topics:**
- Solutions (syrups, elixirs)
- Suspensions
- Emulsions
- Additives (preservatives, buffers, flavors)

**Lab Activities:**
- Prepare oral antibiotic suspension
- Compound flavored solution
- Quality control testing

---

## Module 4: Dosage Forms - Semi-solids

**Week 4**

**Topics:**
- Ointments and ointment bases
- Creams
- Gels
- Pastes
- Incorporation techniques

**Lab Activities:**
- Prepare medicated ointment
- Compound topical cream
- Geometric dilution practice

---

## Module 5: Dosage Forms - Solids

**Week 5**

**Topics:**
- Powders and granules
- Capsules (hard and soft)
- Tablet preparation (limited)
- Suppositories and pessaries

**Lab Activities:**
- Capsule filling
- Powder blending
- Suppository preparation

---

## Module 6: Sterile Compounding

**Week 6**

**Topics:**
- Aseptic technique
- Cleanroom requirements
- Risk levels and categories
- Sterility testing
- Beyond-use dating for sterile preparations

**Lab Activities:**
- Aseptic hand washing
- Garbing procedure
- Sterile product preparation (simulated)
- Media fill testing

---

## Module 7: Quality Assurance

**Week 7**

**Topics:**
- Quality control testing
- Documentation requirements
- Stability considerations
- Beyond-use dating
- Error prevention
- Adverse event reporting

**Activities:**
- Review compounding records
- Perform QC tests
- Stability assessment
- Case study analysis

---

## Module 8: Dispensing and Patient Care

**Week 8**

**Topics:**
- Prescription interpretation
- Labeling requirements
- Patient counseling
- Medication safety
- Professional communication

**Activities:**
- Mock counseling sessions
- Prescription review
- Error case studies
- Final practical examination

---

## Calculation Success

**Master calculations by:**

1. **Practice daily**
   - Work problems every day
   - Don't wait until the quiz

2. **Understand concepts**
   - Don't just memorize formulas
   - Know why it works

3. **Use dimensional analysis**
   - Consistent method
   - Self-checking

4. **Check reasonableness**
   - Does the answer make sense?
   - Is it safe?

---

## Laboratory Success

**Excel in lab by:**

1. **Prepare in advance**
   - Know the procedure
   - Calculate before lab

2. **Work carefully**
   - Quality over speed
   - Follow each step

3. **Document thoroughly**
   - Record everything
   - Complete records

4. **Ask questions**
   - Never guess
   - Instructor is there to help
