# Pharmaceutical Compounding and Dispensing by John F. Marriott

## Book Information

- **Title:** Pharmaceutical Compounding and Dispensing
- **Author:** John F. Marriott, PharmD
- **Edition:** 2nd Edition
- **Publisher:** Pharmaceutical Press, London
- **Year:** 2010
- **ISBN:** 978-0-85369-898-9
- **Pages:** 336
- **Language:** English

## Overview

This comprehensive textbook provides both theoretical foundations and practical guidance for pharmaceutical compounding and dispensing. It is widely used in pharmacy education programs and serves as a valuable reference for practicing pharmacists.

## Target Audience

- Pharmacy students (undergraduate and graduate levels)
- Practicing pharmacists engaged in compounding
- Pharmacy technicians
- Hospital pharmacy staff
- Community pharmacy practitioners

## Key Concepts and Principles

### Quality Assurance in Compounding

1. **Source Quality Ingredients**
   - Use pharmacopeial grade materials (USP, BP, EP)
   - Verify certificates of analysis
   - Proper storage of raw materials

2. **Follow Standard Procedures**
   - Written formulation records
   - Step-by-step procedures
   - Equipment calibration and maintenance

3. **Perform Quality Control**
   - In-process checks
   - Final product testing
   - Visual inspection
   - Weight/volume verification

4. **Maintain Documentation**
   - Master formulation records
   - Compounding records
   - Quality control results
   - Adverse event reporting

### Calculation Principles

The book emphasizes:
- **Accuracy in calculations** - Double-checking all work
- **Appropriate significant figures**
- **Unit awareness** - Always include units
- **Dimensional analysis approach**
- **Practical problem-solving**

### Technique Mastery

Key techniques covered:
- **Levigation** - Creating smooth pastes for incorporation
- **Trituration** - Particle size reduction in mortars
- **Geometric dilution** - Mixing potent drugs safely
- **Incorporation** - Adding drugs to bases uniformly
- **Aseptic technique** - Maintaining sterility
